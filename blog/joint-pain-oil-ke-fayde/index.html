Joint pain oil kya hai

Joint pain oil ek ayurvedic massage oil hai jise jadibootiyon ko base oil me process karke banaya jata hai. Ayurveda me dard ko aksar vata dosha ke badhne se joda jata hai, aur warming (ushna) herbs wale oil ko vata balance karne ke liye use kiya jata hai. Isliye in oils ko abhyanga — matlab sharir par oil massage — ke roop me lagaya jata hai.

Yeh oil bimari ki dawa nahi hai. Yeh massage ke through dard, jakdan aur thakan me aaram deta hai, aur skin ke through aaram ka ehsaas banata hai. Isliye ise daily care ka hissa maana jata hai, ilaaj ka replacement nahi.

Joint pain oil ke 6 fayde

Neeche diye gaye fayde traditional Ayurvedic use par based hain. Har vyakti ka anubhav alag hota hai.

1. Dard me aaram

Nirgundi aur kapoor jaise ingredients massage ke baad halki garmahat dete hain, jisse dard kam mehsoos hota hai aur muscles relax hoti hain.

2. Jakdan (stiffness) kam

Subah uthte hi jodon me jakdan ho to halka massage blood circulation ko support karta hai aur sharir jaldi khulta hai.

3. Muscles ko aaram

Zameen par zyada kaam, gym ya long travel ke baad tight muscles par halka massage thakan kam karne me madad karta hai.

4. Ghutne aur kamar ke liye

Ghutne, kamar, kandhe aur gale me hone wale purane dard me daily massage ek simple aur side-effect-free routine hai.

5. Non-greasy aur quick absorb

Achhe quality ka oil jaldi absorb hota hai aur kapdon par zyada tel nahi chhodta, isliye din me lagana aasan hota hai.

6. Dard ki dawa ke saath support

Doctor ki dawa se pehle ya baad massage routine follow kiya ja sakta hai — lekin dawa band karne se pehle doctor ki salah zaroori hai.

Dard ke saath sujan, laalapan ya garmi lage to yeh sirf massage se theek nahi hoga. Aise case me turant doctor ko dikhayein.

Kaunse ingredients kaam karte hain

Bazaar me har oil ek jaisa nahi hota. Ayurvedic joint pain oil me aam taur par yeh herbs hote hain:

  • Nirgundi — dard aur sujan ke liye sabse zyada use hone wali ayurvedic herb.
  • Gandhapura (Gandhapura / wintergreen type oils) — massage ke baad halki warming feeling, jisse dard me aaram milta hai.
  • Kapoor — cooling-warming contrast se muscles ko aaram, aur halki khushbu.
  • Til (sesame) ya mustard base oil — yeh base oil khud bhi abhyanga ke liye traditional hain aur skin par achhe se absorb hote hain.

Massage ka sahi tarika (step by step)

Oil lagane ka tarika bhi utna hi important hai jitna product ka chunav:

  1. Haath saaf karein aur oil ki 5–8 boondein hathon me lein. Chahiye to ek chhoti bottle ko halke garam paani me rakh kar oil ko thoda gunguna karein.
  2. Ayurveda me abhyanga aksar halka garam kiya jata hai — dard wali jagah par haath ki halki garmahat se shuru karein.
  3. 5–10 minute tak golo kadam me halka pressure dete hue massage karein. Jor se ragdein nahi — dard badh sakta hai.
  4. Kapda laga kar 30 minute tak jodon ko garam rakhein. Rasoi ka halka garam towel bhi kaam karta hai.
  5. Din me 2 baar — subah aur raat ko sone se pehle. Raat ka massage behtar hota hai kyunki sharir rest me hota hai.
  6. Nahane se pehle 30–40 minute ka gap rakhein, taaki oil absorb ho sake.

Saath me chhoti aadatein bhi jodein: roz 15 minute walking, thoda stretching, seedhi chalne me support, aur purahydrate rehna. Massage sirf ek hissa hai — movement aur weight management dard par sabse bada asar dalte hain.

Kitne din me asar dikhta hai

Zyadatar logon ko 3 se 7 din ke regular istemal me halka aaram mehsoos hota hai — khaas kar subah ki jakdan me. Purane dard ke case me 4 se 6 hafte ka regular routine chahiye hota hai.

Yeh expectation rakhein: oil dard kam karne me madad karta hai, lekin cause ko theek nahi karta. Agar:

  • dard 2 hafte se zyada rehta hai ya badhta hai,
  • joड़ me sujan, laalapan ya garmi hai,
  • subah 30 minute se zyada jakdan rehti hai,
  • chot, operation ya ligament injury hui hai,

to doctor se salah lein. Aise cases me X-ray ya physiotherapy ki zaroorat ho sakti hai.

Kis condition me pain oil na lagayein

  • Khuli skin, cut, rash, jalna ya eczema — oil na lagayein.
  • Aankh, naak, muh aur private parts — kapoor wale oil in jagahon par na lagayein, tez jalan hoti hai.
  • Dard ke saath sujan ya bukhar — yeh infection ya chot ka sign ho sakta hai, pehle doctor ko dikhayein.
  • Pregnancy aur breastfeeding — kuch herbs pregnant women ke liye nahi hoti, isliye pehle doctor se puchhein.
  • Allergy — pehle kaan ke peeche halka oil laga kar 24 ghante dekhein.
  • Bachche — sirf doctor ya vaidya ki salah par, aur hamesha diluted form me.
