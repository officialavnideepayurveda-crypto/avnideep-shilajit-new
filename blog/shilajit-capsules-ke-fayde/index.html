Shilajit kya hai

Shilajit ek mineral-rich natural substance hai jo Himalaya ki chattanon se nikalta hai. Ayurveda me ise rasayana kaha jata hai — matlab aisi cheez jo sharir ki energy, immunity aur overall strength ko support karti hai. Traditional texts me shilajit ko shilajatu kaha gaya hai, aur iska Hindi naam hai shilajit ya shilajit stone.

Asli shilajit me fulvic acid aur humic acid ke saath 80 se zyada trace minerals paaye jaate hain. Capsule form me ise standardised dose ke saath pack kiya jata hai, jisse roz sahi matra lena aasan ho jata hai. Bazaar me powder aur resin bhi milta hai, lekin capsule me purity check aur convenience dono behtar hoti hai.

Neeche diye gaye fayde Ayurvedic use aur available research par based hain. Yaad rakhein, shilajit ek supplement hai — kisi bimari ka ilaaj nahi.

Shilajit mitochondria ki activity ko support karta hai, jisse din bhar ki thakan kam mehsoos hoti hai. Jogging, gym ya field work karne wale log ise energy booster ke roop me lete hain.

2. Male vitality

Ayurveda me shilajit ko vrishya rasayana mana gaya hai. Yeh stamina, strength aur reproductive health ko support karne me madadgar hai. Isliye 6Pro jaise formulations me ise main ingredient rakha jata hai.

3. Immunity support

Shilajit ke antioxidants sharir ke natural defence ko support karte hain. Mausam badalne par hone wali thakan aur kamzori me yeh madadgar ho sakta hai.

4. Recovery aur muscle support

Regular exercise karne walon ke liye shilajit recovery aur muscle endurance support kar sakta hai. Iska use traditional vaidyas ne weakness aur dublapan me bhi kiya hai.

5. Brain aur focus

Kuch studies batati hain ki shilajit memory aur focus par positive asar daal sakta hai. Iska reason isme maujood minerals aur fulvic acid ko maana jata hai.

6. Ageing support

Antioxidant properties ke karan shilajit cellular ageing ko slow karne me madadgar mana jata hai. Yeh skin aur baalon ki health ke liye bhi paramparik roop se use hota hai.

Shilajit capsules kaise khaye

Sahi tareeka simple hai, lekin consistency sabse zaroori hai:

  • Dose: Label par likhi dose follow karein — aam taur par din me 1 se 2 capsules.
  • Timing: Subah nashte ke baad ya shaam ko khane ke baad lena sabse aasan hota hai.
  • Ke saath: Capsule ko dudh ya gungune paani ke saath lein. Khali pet lene se bachne me aaram milta hai.
  • Consistency: Roz same samay par lein. Beech me lambe gap se result ruk sakta hai.
  • Diet: Protein-rich diet, 7-8 ghante ki neend aur daily walking se result better hota hai.

Shilajit ko garam doodh ya chai ke saath lena avoid karein, kyunki tez garam se kuch active compounds par asar pad sakta hai. Halka gunguna paani ya normal doodh best hai.

Kitne din me asar dikhta hai

Shilajit turant asar dene wala supplement nahi hai. Ayurveda me rasayana ko 8 se 12 hafte tak regular lena sahi mana jata hai. Zyadatar logon ko 3 se 4 hafte me energy me halka farq mehsoos hota hai, aur 6 hafte ke baad stamina aur recovery me zyada clear change dikhta hai.

Result kai cheezon par depend karta hai — aapki age, diet, neend, activity level aur consistency. Agar aap 2 mahine tak roz lete hain aur phir bhi koi farq nahi mehsoos hota, to apne doctor se salah lein.

Side effects aur savdhaniyan

Sahi dose me shilajit aam taur par safe mana jata hai. Phir bhi kuch logon ko halka gas, acidity, loose motion ya skin rash ho sakta hai. Aise me dose kam karein ya doctor se baat karein.

  • Label ki dose se zyada na lein — zyada matra se stomach upset ho sakta hai.
  • Pregnancy, breastfeeding ya kisi bimari me doctor ki salah ke bina na lein.
  • Diabetic ya BP ki dawa chal rahi ho to pehle apne doctor se confirm karein.
  • Bachchon aur 18 saal se kam umar walon ke liye yeh supplement nahi hai.
  • Asli product hi lein — sasta aur unbranded shilajit me heavy metals ka risk hota hai. Lab-tested product hi choose karein.
