Shilajit Vs Ashwagandha: Kya Antar Hai Aur Kaunsa Le

Dono kya hain

Shilajit ek mineral-rich natural substance hai jo Himalaya ki chattanon se nikalta hai. Ayurveda me ise energy, stamina aur recovery ke liye rasayana mana jata hai. Isme fulvic acid aur 80 se zyada trace minerals hote hain.

Ashwagandha ek jadibooti hai (Withania somnifera) jise adaptogen kaha jata hai — matlab yeh sharir ko stress ke saath adjust hone me madad karti hai. Ayurveda me ise neend, shaanti aur strength ke liye use kiya jata hai.

Kaunsa kab lena chahiye

Shilajit chunein agar:

  • Din bhar thakan aur susti rehti hai, chahe neend poori ho.
  • Gym, running ya heavy physical work ke baad recovery slow lagti hai.
  • Stamina aur overall strength par kaam karna hai.

Ashwagandha chunein agar:

  • Stress zyada hai, dimaag shaant nahi hota.
  • Neend aane me der lagti hai ya neend tooti rehti hai.
  • Exam, job ya business stress ke time focus aur calm chahiye.

Dono problems saath ho sakti hain — aise case me ek se shuru karein, 4–6 hafte dekhein, phir doosra jodein. Ek saath bahut se supplements shuru karna confuse karta hai aur yeh pata nahi chalta ki kaam kya kar raha hai.

Kya dono saath le sakte hain

Haan, zyadatar healthy adults dono saath le sakte hain, kyunki inka mechanism alag hai. Lekin:

  • Har product ki label dose se zyada na lein.
  • Subah shilajit aur raat ko ashwagandha — timing alag rakhein to better.
  • Thyroid, BP, diabetes ki dawa, pregnancy ya breastfeeding me pehle doctor se salah lein.
  • Koi bhi naya supplement shuru karne ke pehle ek list banayein ki aap kya kya le rahe hain.
